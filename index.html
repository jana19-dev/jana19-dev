// About

codepiercer is a Toronto-based web & mobile development studio. We help businesses, founders, and individuals turn ideas into robust, polished software — from fast marketing sites to real-time apps and large-scale platforms — and we love building things people genuinely enjoy using.

With over a decade of experience shipping production software, we bring real engineering rigor to every project: personal and business websites, landing pages and showcases, custom web apps with real-time features, and cross-platform mobile apps for iOS and Android.

We work with you end-to-end — thoughtful design, solid engineering, and reliable, hassle-free hosting — so you get something fast, dependable, and built to last. Have a project in mind? We'd love to hear about it.
